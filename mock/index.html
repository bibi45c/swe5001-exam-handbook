Appendix A — Case Study: GreenCharge EV Charging Platform · 附录 A 案例：GreenCharge 电动车充电平台

1. Background · 背景

The city's energy agency and several private charge point operators (CPOs) are building GreenCharge, a platform on which electric-vehicle (EV) drivers find, reserve, start and pay for public charging points operated by many different CPOs. Phase 1 delivers the platform with two pilot applications: the Driver mobile app and the Operator web portal. Phase 2 adds smart charging and an AI trip assistant.

城市能源局和几家私营充电运营商（CPO）正在建设 GreenCharge 平台。电动车司机在上面查找、预约、启动公共充电桩并付费，这些充电桩由许多不同的运营商经营。第一阶段交付平台和两个试点应用：司机手机 App 和运营商网页门户。第二阶段增加智能充电和 AI 行程助手。

Roles: Driver, CPO Admin, Platform Admin. External systems: Payment Gateway, Grid Operator Price API (publishes the electricity price every 15 minutes), LLM Provider API (Phase 2). About 15,000 charger units are installed in the city; each unit reports its status and meter values to the platform.

角色：司机、运营商管理员、平台管理员。外部系统：支付网关、电网电价 API（每 15 分钟发布一次电价）、LLM 服务商 API（第二阶段）。全市约安装了 15,000 台充电桩，每台都向平台上报状态和电表读数。

3. Extensibility · 可扩展性

The platform shall provide common services and libraries so that future platforms of the agency (e-scooter sharing, battery swapping, public parking) can reuse them. In particular, notification and payment shall be reusable by the agency's other platforms.

平台应提供公共服务和库，让能源局未来的平台（电动滑板车共享、换电、公共停车）可以复用。特别是通知和支付，要能被能源局的其他平台复用。

4. Performance and Scalability · 性能与可扩展性

  • Phase 1 shall support 200,000 registered drivers, with up to 20,000 concurrent drivers at the evening peak (6 pm to 9 pm), 300 CPO Admins and 15,000 charger units.
    第一阶段要支持 20 万注册司机，晚高峰（晚 6 点到 9 点）最多 2 万并发司机，300 名运营商管理员和 15,000 台充电桩。
  • Interactive requests (find, reserve, start, stop) shall complete within 2 seconds for 90% of the requests.
    交互请求（查找、预约、开始、结束）90% 要在 2 秒内完成。
  • A change in charger availability shall be visible to drivers within 10 seconds.
    充电桩可用状态的变化要在 10 秒内让司机看到。
  • The operator dashboard shall be updated every 5 minutes.
    运营商看板每 5 分钟更新一次。
  • Phase 2: when a driver asks the Trip Assistant (UC9), the app shall show that the question has been received within 2 seconds for 90% of the requests; the full answer shall be shown within 15 seconds for 90% of the questions.
    第二阶段：司机询问行程助手（UC9）时，App 90% 要在 2 秒内显示"问题已收到"；90% 的问题要在 15 秒内显示完整答案。
  • Multitenancy is not expected in Phase 1; CPO data is separated by role-based access control.
    第一阶段不要求多租户；运营商数据靠基于角色的访问控制分开。

5. Security · 安全

The platform follows the DMZ / reverse-proxy practice. Personal data is encrypted at rest and in transit and handled according to the PDPA. Payment card data is handled only by the Payment Gateway and by as few platform subsystems as possible (PCI-DSS).

平台遵循 DMZ / 反向代理的做法。个人数据在存储和传输中都加密，并按新加坡个人数据保护法（PDPA）处理。支付卡数据只由支付网关和尽量少的平台子系统处理（PCI-DSS）。

6. Initial Architecture · 初始架构

The logical architecture uses the layers Presentation, Control, Data Access, External Integration and Shared Library. The Driver mobile app (with local storage) and the Operator web portal are the front ends; the backend runs in the cloud.

逻辑架构分为以下几层：Presentation（表现层）、Control（控制层）、Data Access（数据访问层）、External Integration（外部集成层）、Shared Library（共享库层）。司机手机 App（带本地存储）和运营商网页门户是前端；后端运行在云上。

全国艺术与体育管理局正在建设 TixNow，一个由场馆（音乐厅、体育场、剧院）、活动主办方和观众共用的票务平台。平台运行四个应用：

二、模拟卷 2 TixNow：Section A 讲解

Q1(a) 最关键的架构方面（2 分）

三句话拿满： 种子 + 双方 + 交互写一句 → 写一个方面并带上 §3 的数字 → 加一句"做不到会怎样"。常见失分点：写了两个方面（可扩展性 + 多租户），还套用了 PAST2 的"查看服务从缓存读最新值"。

The seeds of the platform are event tickets and seats, produced by organisers and venues and consumed by fans through browsing events, joining the on-sale queue, selecting seats and paying; in resale, fans also act as producers.

平台的种子是活动门票和座位，由主办方和场馆产生，粉丝通过浏览活动、加入开售队列、选座和付款来使用；转售时粉丝也是生产者。

The most crucial architectural aspect is the scalability of the on-sale services: 500,000 fans may join the virtual queue in the first 10 minutes and the platform must handle 50,000 seat-selection requests per minute, so the services must be stateless and scale out automatically, keeping 90% of requests within 2 seconds.

最关键的架构方面是开售服务的可扩展性：开售头 10 分钟可能有 50 万粉丝进入虚拟队列，平台每分钟要处理 5 万次选座请求，所以服务必须无状态、能自动扩容，保持 90% 的请求在 2 秒内完成。

The virtual queue absorbs the peak and admits fans at a rate the seat service can handle, and each seat must be held or sold to only one fan, so the system stays fair. If the on-sale fails or sells a seat twice, fans and organisers lose trust and leave the platform.
